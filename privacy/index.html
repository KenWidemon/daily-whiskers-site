Privacy policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Daily Whiskers is operated by Kenneth Widemon. This policy explains how the app, this website, and support correspondence handle information. For questions or privacy requests, email dailywhiskers.support@gmail.com.

Account information

The app uses Google Firebase email/password authentication. Your email address and password are sent to Firebase to create or authenticate your account. Firebase assigns a user identifier and manages authentication information. The app uses that identifier to recognize your signed-in session.

The app does not write your password to its own files or logs. Firebase's SDK maintains authentication state on your device so you can remain signed in. Logging out and deleting an account are different actions.

Service and security data

Firebase processes technical information such as IP addresses and user-agent information to operate authentication and prevent abuse. Its SDK may also process diagnostic information. See Firebase's privacy information and Apple-platform data disclosures for details.

Your daily content stays local

Cat images and quotes are bundled with the app. The daily card is selected on your device using its local date. Daily Whiskers does not upload your daily-card selection, offer user-content uploads, or maintain a cloud reading history.

Daily Whiskers does not use advertising, sell personal information, or add app-level analytics or crash-reporting services. The app's backend integration is Firebase Authentication; its service-level processing described above still applies.

Why information is used

Account information is used to provide sign-in, account creation, password reset, account deletion, and security. If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and help resolve your request. Do not send passwords, reset links, or verification codes.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, Gmail handles this app's support mailbox, and GitHub Pages hosts this website. Providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country under their applicable terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

This website has no analytics scripts, contact forms, advertising, or embedded third-party media. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email correspondence is processed by Google; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Firebase retains account information until deletion is initiated. Firebase currently states that logged authentication IP addresses are retained for a few weeks and other authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after user deletion is initiated. Account deletion is therefore not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup.

To delete your account, open Settings in the signed-in app and choose Delete Account, verify your current password, and confirm permanent deletion. If your installed version does not show this option or you cannot access the account, contact support for help. Uninstalling the app or logging out does not delete the Firebase account.

Support messages are held in the operator's Gmail mailbox separately from Firebase account data. You can contact us about access to or deletion of that correspondence.

Your choices and requests

You can reset your password from the sign-in screen, log out from Settings, or request account deletion. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights over your personal information. Contact the address above to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify account ownership without asking for your password. You may also contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please contact us if anything about the app's handling of your information is unclear.
