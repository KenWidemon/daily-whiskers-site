Daily Whiskers support

Questions about your daily cat moment? Contact Kenneth Widemon at dailywhiskers.support@gmail.com.

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots can help, but remove personal information first. Never send passwords, reset links, or verification codes.

I cannot sign in

Check your email address and internet connection. If you have forgotten your password, enter your account email on the sign-in screen and choose Forgot password? Check your inbox and spam folder, then complete the emailed reset link before trying the new password.

My daily card has not changed

The card follows the local date on your device. Return to the app after the date changes to refresh it. Check that your device's date and time are correct. The collection rotates, so cards can appear again.

How do I delete my account?

  1. Sign in and open the Settings menu using the gear icon.
  2. Choose Delete Account and enter your current password.
  3. Confirm Delete Permanently. Successful deletion signs you out.

Deletion is permanent. Logging out or uninstalling is not account deletion. If the option is missing in your installed version, deletion fails, or you cannot sign in, email support for assistance. We may need to verify ownership; do not send your password. See the privacy policy for provider retention details.

Privacy questions

Use the same support address for questions about your information or to request access, correction, or deletion. Please describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive information.
